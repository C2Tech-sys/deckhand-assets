Deckhand · joindeckhand.com

Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Deckhand is a private, invitation-only web application operated by C2 Technology Systems ("we", "us") at joindeckhand.com. It watches the prices of cruise purchases you have already made on your own Royal Caribbean account and alerts you when a lower price is available. This policy explains what information Deckhand handles and how.

Who can use Deckhand

Only people invited by the operator. There is no public sign-up. Deckhand is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Royal Caribbean International or Celebrity Cruises.

Information you provide

  • Your Deckhand account: name, email address, and a password (stored only as a salted hash).
  • Your Royal Caribbean login, if you add one. It is stored encrypted at rest and used solely to sign in to Royal Caribbean on your behalf to read the prices of your own bookings and purchases. It is never displayed again, shared, or used for any other purpose.
  • Your mobile number, if you turn on text alerts, and your alert preferences.

Information Deckhand collects automatically

  • The prices, booking details, and purchase details that Royal Caribbean returns for your own bookings, kept as a history so you can see how prices have moved.
  • Standard technical logs (request times, errors) needed to run the service.

Text messages (SMS)

If you turn on text alerts and provide your mobile number, Deckhand will send you price-drop alerts and occasional test or service messages at that number. Message frequency varies — typically a few messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop at any time by turning text alerts off in Setup → Notifications or by replying STOP to any message; reply HELP for help. Your mobile number is used only to send you these alerts. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Email

If you turn on email alerts, Deckhand sends alerts to the email address on your account through our email delivery provider (SMTP2Go). You can turn email alerts off at any time.

Service providers

To deliver alerts we use Telnyx (text messages) and SMTP2Go (email). These providers process only what is needed to deliver a message to you. Deckhand itself runs on infrastructure controlled by the operator; your data is not stored with any other third party.

How long we keep information

For as long as you have a Deckhand account. When your account is removed, your login details, Royal Caribbean credentials, and mobile number are deleted. Price history for your bookings may be retained in anonymized form.

Security

Credentials are encrypted at rest; the service is reachable only over HTTPS; access requires a personal login.

Children

Deckhand is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do. Questions: support@c2techsys.com.
